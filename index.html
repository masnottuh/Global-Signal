ABOUT

Mathematical Rigor, Not Black Boxes

We believe in open and verifiable models. Every signal we produce can be verified, reproduced, and understood.

Our Mission

Global Signal exists to democratize access to high-quality financial analysis. We ingest vast streams of data from global sources and transform them into actionable predictive signals.

This empowers traders, investors, and institutions to anticipate market movements, mitigate risks, and seize opportunities with greater precision and speed than otherwise possible.

Our Approach

We aggregate and process data feeds from diverse global sources; news, social media, economic indicators, satellite imagery, and alternative datasets. This is done in an efficient and robust fashion, deliberately avoiding unknowable "black-box" machine learning models.

Raw information is distilled into high-fidelity signals that forecast trends, volatility, and asset performance across equities, commodities, forex, and cryptocurrencies.

Academically Rigorous

All models based on established mathematical and statistical principles. Fully documented and peer-reviewable.

Diverse Data Sources

News, social media, economic indicators, satellite imagery, and alternative datasets processed in real-time.

Open & Reproducible

No hidden algorithms. Every signal derivation is knowable and can be independently verified.

AI-Ready Features

Ideal input variables for training advanced AI models, charting, or performing quantitative analysis.

Our Commitment: We are in the business of separating signal from noise—not by guessing, surface-fitting, or machine learning, but by applying known and understood mathematical and statistical concepts in an academic fashion.
